01 / Our story

It began with
a journey.
And a simple thought.

Travelling across India, especially through its villages, Santan Ranjan noticed something special: every village had its own unique food items, with flavours and stories that belonged to that place.

Why should these discoveries stay in one place? That question became Gaonkheda — a brand from Bihar with a vision to bring village food products online and make them available across the country.

Our story is about connecting people with the tastes of India’s villages, one discovery at a time.

SR
Santan RanjanFounder, Gaonkheda · Bihar, India
